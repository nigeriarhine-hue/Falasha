# GOD SENT dad hat (matches the GOD SENT Christian graphic tee)

Blank: Printful Classic Dad Hat, Yupoong 6245CM (about $14.02 base cost).
Placement: front embroidery, centred. Files are 1500 × 525 px, transparent PNG, 300 DPI (5" × 1.75").
The logo itself stitches at about **2.2" wide × 1.75" tall**.

## The logo (matches the GOD SENT shirt)
- "GOD" in white brush caps, with "SENT" in gold underneath and offset to the right.
- A gold brush cross whose upright doubles as the T in SENT.
- A gold brush swoosh underline, and a forward slant on everything.
- Font: Knewave (SIL Open Font License, free for commercial use).
- Brush edges are kept solid, with no dry-brush gaps or hairlines, so it stitches cleanly. The thinnest part is well over Printful's 0.05" minimum.

## Printful setup
1. Product Templates → Classic Dad Hat (Yupoong 6245CM) → Embroidery → Front.
2. Upload the `god-sent-hat-front_…` file and centre it. Don't scale it beyond the 1.75" height.
3. Set the thread colours as above. Printful usually charges a one-time digitization fee per design.
