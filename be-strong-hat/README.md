# BE STRONG and of Good Courage dad hat

Blank: Printful Classic Dad Hat, Yupoong 6245CM. Placement: front embroidery, centred.
The design stitches at about **4.7" wide × 1.75" tall**.

## Design
- Text only: "BE STRONG" in bold white brush capitals, with "and of Good Courage" in white script underneath.
- The lettering has a black outline so it reads on every band.
- Behind it are three brush-edged bands in African colours, in two colourways:
  - **Red / Gold / Green**: best on black hats, and also good on white, stone and khaki.
  - **Red / Black / Green** (Pan-African flag): best on white, stone and khaki. On a black hat the black band blends into the hat.
- Fonts: Knewave and Pacifico (SIL Open Font License, free for commercial use).

## Stitch check
`tools/stitch-width-check.py` marks anything thinner than Printful's 0.05" minimum at hat size.
- The white lettering and the black outline pass.
- The only flags are thin slivers of band colour between the outlines. These sit on the background layer, which is stitched first as a full fill underneath, so they're fine.

## Notes before you order
- This design is a solid patch of stitching about 4.7" wide. That makes the front of the hat stiffer and heavier than a text-only design, which is normal for this style.
- If Printful's placement tool says it's too wide for the front, scale it down to about 4.5" wide.
- The KJV wording is "Be strong and **of a** good courage" (Joshua 1:9). This design uses your wording, "and of Good Courage".

## Printful setup
1. Product Templates → Classic Dad Hat (Yupoong 6245CM) → Embroidery → Front.
2. Upload a `PRINTFUL-UPLOAD_…` file and centre it.
3. Match the thread colours to the table above.
4. Check Printful's stitch preview before saving. Printful usually charges a one-time digitization fee per design.
